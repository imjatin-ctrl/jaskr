Why Jaskr GymGrid? Gap Analysis

An objective comparison of Jaskr GymGrid against commercial fitness platforms (ClassPass, Mindbody) and traditional single-gym websites.

Honest Evaluation Note

Commercial platforms like Mindbody provide specialized enterprise ERP, while ClassPass focuses on boutique fitness credits. Jaskr GymGrid's differentiator is the combination, not any single feature: combining consumer city-wide discovery, rule-based cross-branch access arbitration (D2), transparent historical crowd density modeling (D3), and strict multi-tenant relational financial accounting (D4) in an open, auditable academic DBMS.

Project Scope & Academic Assumptions

  • Fictional Demonstration Dataset: All member identities, phone numbers, email addresses, and financial transactions are deterministically generated by seed_data.py. No real personal or banking data is stored.
  • Simulated Payments: Payments are processed via internal transaction workflows (using sp_record_payment) with simulated gateways (UPI, Credit Card, Cash) without connecting to third-party payment rails.
  • Deterministic Snapshot Time: The demo environment operates in a deterministic timeline centered at 2026-10-30 10:00:00 IST to ensure all attendance patterns, expiring memberships, and variance reports stay coherent.
  • Relational 3NF Schema: The database enforces 3rd Normal Form with clean foreign keys, composite indexes, integrity triggers, and relational views.
