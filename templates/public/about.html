DBMS Concepts Demonstrated

• DDL: 24 Tables with strict PK/FK, ON DELETE CASCADE/SET NULL, and CHECK constraints.

• DML: Parameterized INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE queries protecting against SQL injection.

• Joins: Inner, Left Outer, and Multi-table joins across 5+ relations.

• Aggregation: GROUP BY with HAVING thresholds on financial summaries.

• Views: 5 Relational views (v_active_memberships, v_public_branch_directory, etc.).

• Transactions: Stored procedures with ACID isolation, exception handlers, and ROLLBACK.

• DCL: Dedicated MySQL users (gg_public, gg_member, etc.) with least privilege.

• Indexes: B-Tree composite indexes justified for timestamp ranges and foreign keys.
