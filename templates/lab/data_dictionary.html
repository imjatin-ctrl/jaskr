Data Dictionary & 3NF Normalization

Exhaustive relational schema specification across all 24 tables, including Primary Keys, Foreign Keys, CHECK constraints, and formal Third Normal Form (3NF) proofs.

1NF

First Normal Form

Atomicity Guaranteed: Every column contains atomic (indivisible) values. No comma-separated equipment, hours, or facilities lists. Multi-valued amenities and operational days are decomposed into independent child tables (branch_facilities, branch_hours).

2NF

Second Normal Form

Full Functional Dependency: No non-prime attribute depends on a partial subset of any candidate key. In composite-key relations like branch_hours(branch_id, day_of_week) and branch_access_rules(plan_id, branch_id), attributes depend strictly on the whole key.

3NF

Third Normal Form

No Transitive Dependencies: Zero non-key transitive dependencies ($X \to Y$). Member profiles reference home_branch_id without duplicating branch address or city; cash transactions in payments link to invoice_id without duplicating line-item tax details.
