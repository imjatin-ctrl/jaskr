Relational Design & 3NF Justification

The Jaskr GymGrid database strictly adheres to Third Normal Form (3NF). Every non-key attribute is non-transitively dependent on the primary key:

  • 1NF: All attribute values are atomic; repeating groups (facilities, equipment, hours) are modeled in separate child relations.
  • 2NF: No partial functional dependencies on composite keys (e.g. branch_hours depends fully on (branch_id, day_of_week)).
  • 3NF: No transitive dependencies; member demographics reside in members, user credentials in users, and branch details in branches.
